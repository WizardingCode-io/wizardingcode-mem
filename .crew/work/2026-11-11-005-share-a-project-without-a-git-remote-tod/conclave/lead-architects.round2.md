## Notes
- The identity leaf is sound. `src/core/identity.ts` with no imports can be imported by `src/sharing/levels.ts`, `src/team/*` and `src/sync/naming.ts` without crossing any boundary. `tests/unit/sync-boundary.test.ts:98-107` restricts only `src/sync` into `src/team` (only `contract.ts` is allowed) and `src/team` into `src/sync`. `team-store-boundary.test.ts:31-46` forbids only `store/` and `bun:sqlite` imports. Neither test constrains `core/`, so the claim at spec.md:89 and AC-1 that the boundary tests "allow" the leaf is true but needs no test change. Reword it, so nobody edits the tests for nothing.
- The prefilter inventory matches the code: `src/ui/api.ts:92`, `src/sync/inbound.ts:664`, and `src/sharing/report.ts:182` plus its `isHostLedAlias` filters at `:187` and `:196`. `src/core/project.ts:87` and `src/links/suggest.ts:42` stay `remote:` by meaning, which is a stated non-goal. `src/sharing/policy.ts:159` has no prefilter. `effectiveLevel` at `levels.ts:155` is where `isHostLedAlias` has to become `isTeamAlias`, and the spec covers it.
- The `resolveProject` upsert (`src/core/project.ts:132-137`) only reassigns `path:` and `gitdir:` aliases. A `name:` row is therefore never moved or lost behind the person's back, which backs the spec's claim that a name is lost only by `forgetName`.
- `src/modules/knowledge/resolve.ts:29-37` ranks `name:` as 3 and not strong, so the knowledge side needs no change, as the spec's non-goal says.
- The data model holds. Team migration 1 has no trigger or view over `remote_alias`. `RENAME COLUMN` carries the `changes_scope` index, and `openTeamForWrite` (`src/team/db.ts:107-119`) already refuses to migrate under a fresh lease. Core `LATEST_VERSION` is `MIGRATIONS.length` (`src/store/db.ts:29`) and `openDb` passes it as `latest` (`:474`), so a 0.7.0 binary does refuse a version 8 file. Putting migration 8 in the core list rather than the sharing namespace, as part 6 did for its core `memories` indexes, is deliberate (D-5) and its alternative is faced at spec.md:217. The spec says CLAUDE.md's "`user_version` stays 7" sentences change.
- The client's own `remote_alias` columns (`knowledge_received`, `sharing_received_memories`) keep their names, and the spec's scope is the team's four columns. Say so in ADR 0028 so the two vocabularies are not read as a missed rename.
- The contract design holds against the code. `auth.ts:47-48` already tests `ACCEPTED_CONTRACTS.includes(header)` and answers 409 with the list. `SyncClient` already takes a `contract` option (`client.ts:134-150`), and `servers.ts` and `join.ts` already store the column. A 0.7.0 client gets a `movedTo` it cannot parse and takes the delete path (`inbound.ts:197-205`, `readsRange` returning false on `parseEntryUid` null). The spec's description of that behaviour is accurate. The silent loss of that copy is accepted in the risks section.
- Hooks stay off the network. The `/v1/me` refresh sits in `startSteps` inside the detached sync process, and the new viewer routes and CLI call the server, never a hook.
- The alternatives are faced and the rejections are reasoned: no code at all, emergent slugs, a server-minted id, a rename as a move, `name:` as a manifest strong alias, and a remote list disclosure. ADR 0028 is a free number, and the amendments to ADR 0025 D-16, ADR 0026 and ADR 0027 are named.
