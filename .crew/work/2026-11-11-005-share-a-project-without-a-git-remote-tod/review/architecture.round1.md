## Findings
- F-1 · defect · src/team/routes.ts:546 (with src/team/contract.ts:107) · The change raises the contract to 2. The refusal shape stays pinned as `["error", "instance", "serverId"]`, but `POST /v1/names` now answers 409 `{ error: "exists", name: <NameItemShape> }`. The client relies on that extra key: `commitBinding` in src/sync/binding.ts reads `made.body.name` to build the join state, and the dock re-opens the join confirmation from it. The base says two things about this. First, the contract is the key lists and result shapes that `CONTRACT_FINGERPRINT` pins. Second, ADR 0027's Known limits (docs/adr/0027-sync-client.md:102) says the refusal shape leaves out `accepted` (which the server sends and the client reads) and promises that "the next contract bump pins the one and drops the other". This change is that bump. It does neither, and it adds one more body key the client reads but the fingerprint does not cover. Rubric question 3: a contract changed without its file. Fix: within this bump, pin the 409 bodies the client reads in `RESULT_SHAPES`. That means `accepted` on the contract refusal and the `exists` body with `name`. One way is a `nameConflict` shape, `["error", "instance", "name", "serverId"]`, with `RefusalShape` given `name?: NameItemShape`. Also drop the unused `retryAfterSec` as ADR 0027 said, repin the fingerprint, and change ADR 0027's sentence to past tense. The other way is to state in ADR 0028 that the debt carries forward, and why.
- F-2 · advisory · src/core/identity.ts:10 · Nothing reads `ALIAS_KINDS`. The Design says "a new kind is one entry", and ADR 0028 and the leaf's own comment say the same, but each team rule lists the two kinds by hand: `isTeamAlias`, `TEAM_ALIAS_SQL`, `stripTeamPrefix`, `PREFIXES` and `heldByContract` in src/sync/servers.ts:432. Either drive `isTeamAlias` and `TEAM_ALIAS_SQL` from the table, or remove the "one entry" claim from the leaf comment and the ADR.
- F-3 · advisory · src/cli/commands/share.ts:521 · The "names held" rule is written out again with a literal `2` in three places: here, in src/sync/state.ts:521 and in ui/src/components/ProjectsView.vue:161. The base already has one predicate (`heldByContract` in servers.ts) and one reader (`ServerStatus.namesHeld`, which ADR 0027 says is what status, doctor and the viewer read). `share status` should read `statusReport(...).namesHeld` for its server, or call `heldByContract`, instead of recomputing it.
- F-4 · advisory · ui/src/components/ProjectsView.vue:163 · `slugOfAlias` strips a literal `/^name:/`. The Design's reason for having the leaf was to remove literal prefixes. The page already imports src/sharing/names.ts, so it can import `NAME_PREFIX` or `stripTeamPrefix` from src/core/identity.ts (a leaf with no imports) as well.
- F-5 · advisory · src/team/contract.ts:255 · `FeedItemShape.nameId` is typed as required (`string | null`), but a contract-1 server (the 0.7.0 image this client must keep talking to) never sends it. `recreatedName` in inbound.ts copes today only because names are held at contract 1. Type it `nameId?: string | null`, or normalise `undefined` to `null` where the page is read, so the type describes what both contracts send.
- F-6 · advisory · src/team/routes.ts:73 · The Design puts `listNames(after, limit)` "with the counts" and `nameItemOf` in store.ts. The build lists plain rows (`listLiveNames`) and puts each item together in routes.ts, with `nameCountsOf` and `memberById` called per row. That is two or three queries per name, up to 200 a page, against the spec's sizing of range scans per page. No SQL left store.ts, so no boundary is crossed, but the Design was read differently and ADR 0028's "What building changed" does not record it.
- F-7 · advisory · src/sharing/names.ts:11 · names.ts imports a new knowledge-module export, `slugCore` (src/modules/knowledge/entry.ts). The Design named `slugify`, and ADR 0028's "What building changed" does not mention the new export. CLAUDE.md (the "Shared names" bullet) calls names.ts a leaf, but it imports entry.ts. Record the export in ADR 0028 and change "leaves" to fit.
- F-8 · advisory · src/sharing/store.ts:569 · `setLevel` and `forgetName` now insert and delete `project_aliases` rows, a table CLAUDE.md gives to the engine (`src/core/project.ts`). The Design asks for this and the claude-mem importer already writes that table directly, so it is not drift from the spec. But ADR 0028 never says that the sharing store now writes the engine's alias table. One sentence in "The name of a project" would make that ownership explicit.
