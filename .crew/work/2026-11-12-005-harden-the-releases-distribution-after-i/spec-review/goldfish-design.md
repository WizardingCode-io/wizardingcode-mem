## Findings
- F-1 · Major · Problem, item 1 · The spec says as fact that Actions "does not know this encoded form" but gives no source, and the actions/runner secret masker registers base64 value encoders, including shifted alignments; because "x-access-token:" is 15 bytes (a multiple of 3), the token's own base64 appears aligned inside the header's base64, so the runner may already mask most of it; the premise needs a check against the runner's masker or a real run, because it decides whether the mask line (Design 1) is new protection or just a new place where the full credential is printed.
- F-2 · Major · Design 1, the mask line / Risks · On a runner the design adds a line that prints the whole encoded credential to standard output on purpose, and it is hidden only if the runner treats it as a command; the spec names step debug but not the other ways that line can reach the log as plain text: a later `::stop-commands::`, a workflow change that pipes or captures the step's stdout (`| tee`, `$(...)`), or a re-run with "Enable debug logging"; a reader would not see that G-1's one allowed exception is itself an exposure that the redaction does not cover, and no AC or rule in the documents (AC-15) tells a later change to keep push.ts's stdout uncaptured on a runner.
- F-3 · Nit · Non-goals vs Design 1 and Design 2 · "Any change to `gitEnv`" is a non-goal and Design 2 says "`gitEnv` is not changed", but Design 1 has `gitEnv` build its header from `encodedCredential`; that is a change to gitEnv (even if it gives the same output), so the text contradicts itself and should say "no change to its output".
- F-4 · Nit · J-4 · J-4 says a second run for the same tag writes nothing new and only names the "A bad release" exception; it leaves out the runbook's other refusal ("Only the highest release can be repeated: an older tag is refused"), so a hand start after a later tag has been cut is refused, not idempotent; separately, "look again after a minute" has no backing for why one minute is enough to show a late-accepted dispatch.
- F-5 · Nit · G-3 · "ends within five minutes" overstates `timeout-minutes`, which counts only from when the job starts on a runner, not while it waits in the queue; the goal should say "runs for at most five minutes".
- F-6 · Nit · Acceptance criteria, AC-18 · "names piece 2" uses a term the spec never defines (Design 4 says "points to the next item"), so the check a test would make on the findings row is unclear.
- F-7 · Nit · Acceptance criteria · The ids run out of order (AC-9..12, AC-6, AC-13, AC-18, AC-15, AC-16, AC-17) and the spec explains the retired ids; this makes it harder to map criteria to Design sections.
- F-8 · Nit · Heilmeier 6 and 7 · The spec states no cost or duration (effort, review rounds, and that the change is proved only by the next real tag), and no midway check apart from the ACs; the risks say runner behaviour "is seen only in a real run" but name no point at which someone will look at that run's log and summary for the encoded value.
