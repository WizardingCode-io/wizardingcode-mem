## Findings
- F-1 · Major · spec.md:111 (The hooks), also spec.md:205 and AC-17 spec.md:179 · The text says the hook's spawn decision costs "one read", but the same sentence lists three: the `count(*)` over `sharing_servers`, the lease and the stop flag. Someone writing AC-17's "through one read" test would either count three reads and fail it, or merge the three into one query the spec never describes. Either name the single query that returns all three values, or write "three reads" in all three places.
- F-2 · Major · spec.md:101 (The policy per server) · "which stops their sweeps, and is safe" asserts safety and gives no consequence. By the rule at spec.md:115, a changed epoch makes every memory a candidate on every server, so editing one server's lists triggers a full memory sweep on all servers. A reader would miss that cost. State the consequence (a full memory re-check on every server, bounded by the batch limits) in place of "is safe".
- F-3 · Major · spec.md:146 (Restore of the client store) · The restore's step order sits in one sentence of about 600 words. Its anchors are unclear: "the same step, in the raw copy before the rename" comes after the post-rename steps, and it is not clear whether "the same step" means `resetLevels` or something else. An implementer could clear the stale `sharing.carry_owed`/`carry_done` marks or reset the levels at the wrong point relative to the rename and `openDb`. Rewrite as a numbered list of steps (before the rename, the rename, after the rename, the failure branches), one action per step.
- F-4 · Major · spec.md:123 (The inbound side) · The unbind reconcile, the start's `reindexReceived`, their crash cases and the re-bind cases are all one sentence chained by semicolons. The order these steps run in relative to a pull's first page, which the earlier rounds kept returning on, has to be pieced together from parentheses. "until the reconcile the rows read as before" does not say which reads see which rows. Split it into an ordered list: what runs at the sync's start, what runs before a pull's first page, and what each crash leaves behind.
- F-5 · Major · spec.md:80 (Where things live, `ledger.ts`) · "every read of them but the two read-only reads … and but the restore's carry, `completeOwedCarry` …" is the exception list the boundary test is built from, and "and but" makes the exceptions hard to count. A reader could allow too much or too little in the test. List the exceptions to `ledger.ts`'s ownership as a separate bullet list of files and functions.
- F-6 · Major · AC-27 spec.md:189 · "the one-time copy … is read beside the budgets" sets no pass bar, so nobody can say whether this criterion passed or failed. State what is measured (the store size, the copy's wall time) and what result fails it, or say it is recorded with no threshold. Also say what happens when the copy outlasts the 10 s other openers wait (spec.md:81).
