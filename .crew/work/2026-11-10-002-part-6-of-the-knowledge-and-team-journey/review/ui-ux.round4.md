## Findings
- F-1 · defect · ui/src/components/settings/Servers.vue:261 · /settings#servers held and muted at 360, a mandatory width that AC-90 names, in both schemes. The row's action group is `flex flex-none flex-wrap`. Because it cannot shrink, its buttons never wrap and run past the card's edge. In held (Resume…, Mute, Rejoin…, Remove…), Remove… is cut off entirely, so the row offers no way to remove the server. In muted, the label is clipped to "Remov". In listed, token-missing and stopped, Remove… sits flush against the row border. This is a hidden control at a mandatory width (review/ui/settings-held@360-dark.png, review/ui/settings-held@360-light.png, review/ui/settings-muted@360-dark.png, review/ui/settings-muted@360-light.png, review/ui/settings-listed@360-dark.png)
