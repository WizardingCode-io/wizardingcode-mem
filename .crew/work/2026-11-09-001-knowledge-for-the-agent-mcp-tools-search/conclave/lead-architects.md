- R-1 · .crew/work/2026-11-09-001-knowledge-for-the-agent-mcp-tools-search/spec.md:106 (and plan.md:13, plan.md:46) · The spec says memory's `hasEvidence` is "extracted" into `hasEvidence(query, fields, rules)` and that memory calls it "with its fields unchanged". `src/retrieve/prompt.ts:339` does not allow that. Memory's function is `hasEvidence(candidate, query, isRare)`. It uses a weighted rule: `EVIDENCE_WEIGHT`, rarity through `isRare` (a database lookup), `candidate.overlap` from session files, and one test for identifiers. The spec's three rules are different: a code name, three distinct shared words, and two title words plus one. Those rules are the withdrawn ADR 0020 floor, not memory's. No signature with `fields` and `rules` can reproduce the weighted rule and `isRare` without changing memory's behaviour, and the pinned snapshots would then fail. Choose one of two fixes and state it in spec.md and plan.md. Either extract only what both modules truly share (the identifier test, matching by stem, the generic-word filter), with each module deciding on top of it. Memory's `isRare` and `overlap` rule then stay in `prompt.ts`, and the "no second copy" rationale is narrowed to match. Or withdraw the claim of a single evidence function, and say that knowledge's floor lives in `policy.ts` and uses the shared primitives. Plan item 5 must list which of `tests/unit/prompt-matches.test.ts`'s cases pass unchanged through the new function. It must also say how `isRare` and `overlap` are carried.
