## Findings
- F-1 · Major · Open Questions, line 35 · "the second changes the session-start section, CUJ 1, AC-3 and the first risk, and nothing else". This is false as written. The second answer would also change Goals bullet 2 ("At every session start…"), D-9's row in the Decisions table, and the first bullet of Alternatives rejected, which rejects that same answer. If the person picks the second answer, a reader who edits only the four places listed leaves the Goals and the Alternatives saying the opposite of the design.
- F-2 · Major · Non-Functional Requirements, Rollout, line 200 · "the first drain rebuilds the repositories whose head and fingerprint the migration cleared, and until then `pinned` is 0 everywhere, so the hooks show no pinned entry before that drain". This conflicts with line 113 in two ways. First, a drain refreshes "at most 3 stale repositories per drain", so one drain does not rebuild all of them. Second, a tool's `ensureFresh` also fills `pinned`, so a tool call can come before any drain. The rollout reader would expect pins to appear everywhere after one drain. They would miss that with more than 3 repositories some pins wait for later drains, and that with distillation off only a tool call or `knowledge rebuild` fills them.
- F-3 · Major · Risks and rollback, "A stale index on the hook path", line 187 · "shown only after the next drain rebuilds the repository (seconds to minutes)". The time is not measured and does not hold in every case. With `WIZARDINGCODE_MEM_DISTILL=off` no drain runs (line 113), and the cap of 3 repositories per drain can push a repository to a later drain. A reader of the risk would bound staleness at minutes and miss that it can be indefinite until a tool call or a manual rebuild.
- F-4 · Major · The judgements, Heuristic, line 138, with the rules on line 141 · The heuristic judge is given relation labels ("`same` at 0.6 or more, `related` at 0.3"), but the duplicate rule tests `relationScore ≥ 1.5`. The spec never says what `relationScore` the heuristic emits for each label. AC-10 implies Jaccard 0.6 makes a duplicate, but an implementer has to guess the mapping (for example same=2, related=1). A different guess changes which proposals are refused.
- F-5 · Major · What the agent is shown, Prompt, rule 3, line 111 · "two of the title's own non-generic words plus one more word of the entry". The spec does not say whether the third word must also be non-generic or outside `STOPWORDS`. Read literally, any word counts, stopwords included, which widens the floor that the probe's "at most 1 of 28" target is meant to hold. A reader would build the looser rule.
- F-6 · Nit · Acceptance criteria, AC-4, line 169 · "three telling words". "Telling" is defined nowhere. The design's term is "three distinct non-generic words" (rule 2), so the AC should use the design's term.
- F-7 · Nit · Goals, line 12 · "by an exact lexical rule". The rule matches words by stem (line 111), so "exact" says something the design does not do. "Fixed" or "deterministic" may be what is meant.
- F-8 · Nit · The judgements, line 137, and AC-10 · "one Score", "one Noul", "a Noul of 0.8". These are TypeSafe's answer kinds, and the spec never explains them, so a fresh reader cannot tell what a Noul answer is or what range it has. Line 139 has the same problem: "like `personal`" names a method with no gloss.
- F-9 · Nit · Open Questions and Decisions, lines 30-35 · "the session", "the crew's validator", "`stated-default`", "`crew decide`, relayed". This is process jargon that a reader of the feature spec has to decode before they can see what is still open: the person has not yet confirmed D-9 in their own words.
- F-10 · Nit · Open Questions, line 32 · "memory gives up to 840 characters of its 4,200". By F5′ (line 79), memory also loses 2 characters per accepted contribution, so the most it gives up is 842. This is a small imprecision next to a number that is otherwise checkable.
- F-11 · Nit · Migration 7, line 94 · The paragraph is one sentence of about 250 words with nested parentheses. It mixes DDL, trigger semantics, a performance target and rollback. The 2 s rebuild target and the "only active, problem-free rows are indexed" rule are easy to miss inside it. Line 98 ("Both contributions share: …") and line 111 have the same shape.
- F-12 · Nit · Prompt, line 111 · "Host prompts (`isFromTheHost`) and prompts `buildQuery` finds too little in get nothing." This is a garden-path sentence: the reader parses "finds too little in" before reaching the verb. In the same paragraph, "(18 entries, 12 relevant and 28 unrelated prompts)" reads at first as if the 12 and 28 were entries.
- F-13 · Nit · Goals, line 13 · "…`knowledge_propose` (a change is …); inside a project, and `search` and `get` with `--global` over …". The semicolon cuts the sentence where the scope of the tools begins, so the clause reads as a fragment. Line 11 has a similar problem: "stays byte for byte with no entries" is missing "the same".
- F-14 · Nit · Session start, line 109 · "Every session start, every project (D-9)". This sentence fragment repeats what the first sentence of the paragraph and Goals bullet 2 already say.
- F-15 · Nit · What the agent is shown, line 107 · "strips `<wizardingcode-knowledge>` blocks and its line shapes too". "Its" has no clear antecedent after the plural "blocks".
- F-16 · Nit · Acceptance criteria, lines 173-182 · The criteria run AC-7, 8, 16, 9 … 12, 14, 17, 15, 13. The superseded AC-8 and AC-14 stay as stubs that point to text "in the item's record only", which this document's reader cannot find. AC-11's compact clause ("rows of the sessions it removes") is also narrower than AC-15 and line 147 (orphans by session or by repository), so two criteria state the same behaviour differently.
- F-17 · Nit · The tools, line 129 · "the per-call judge and the rate counter are created lazily on the first `run`". If they are created once, on the first run, the judge is per server, not per call. The name contradicts the lifetime.
- F-18 · Nit · Alternatives rejected, lines 152 and 155 · "the share holds a handful of lines" and "unbounded cost". The first is vague where a measure exists (840 characters at session start). The second is a claim the spec does not support, since a rerank's calls could be capped like the propose path's.
- F-19 · Nit · Freshness, line 113 · "When a repository's recorded head or fingerprint differ from the disk" should be "differs", since "or" makes the subject singular. AC-5 has the same agreement error ("whose head or fingerprint differ").
