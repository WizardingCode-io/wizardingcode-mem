# architects lead's validation of 2026-11-09-002-sharing-policy-per-project-local-only-kn: spec
verdict: returned
R-1 · plan.md Phase B step 4 and Phase C step 6, with spec.md "Storage" `setLevel` step 3 and "The policy and `decide`": `setLevel`, `previewLevel` and `reconcileLevels` live in `src/sharing/store.ts` (Phase B) and call `levelOf` and take a `SharingPolicy`, but `levelOf` and `loadPolicy` are created in `policy.ts` in Phase C. `loadPolicy` in turn needs `counterOf`/`settingsSeen` from `store.ts`. That is a circular import, and Phase B cannot typecheck on its own. Step 3's cases that need a loaded policy, such as an `unbound-alias` row, `disabled` and the rebind case, also need the Phase C loader. Move `levelOf` (and the policy-reading helpers) to a leaf that both files import, such as `types.ts` or a small `levels.ts`, and have step 3 build its policies with a hand helper. Alternatively, move the policy-taking functions of `store.ts` into Phase C. The policy-free store (counter, generation, change log, `resetLevels`, `applySettingsPatch`) stays in B.
R-2 · spec.md D-11 and Storage "Received items", with plan.md step 5 (the `imported` memory cases): the guard that keeps a received memory from leaving is `origin = 'imported'`, a value the engine already acts on. `src/backfill/replace.ts:55` archives `origin = 'imported'` memories in `history replace-imports`, and `src/judge/rejudge.ts:58,68` rejudges them and can archive them. A memory received from the team would be archived or rejudged as if it came from claude-mem. This contract is built here, so the spec must either (a) name the collision and require that part 6 restricts those two paths to claude-mem imports, with a note in the ADR, or (b) use a guard that does not overload `imported` (a ledger-only guard plus a distinct origin or marker decided here). Say which, so the decision is made once.
