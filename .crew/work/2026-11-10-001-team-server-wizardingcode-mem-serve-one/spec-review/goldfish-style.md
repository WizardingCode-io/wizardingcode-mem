## Findings
- F-1 · Major · spec.md, Members/Authentication (line 157) against D-19 and AC-31 · The refusals are listed in the order "401 missing or wrong token, 401 device revoked, or 403 member disabled", and D-19 and the disable route revoke every device of a disabled member. Read in that order, a disabled member's device always gets 401 `device revoked`, so the 403 `member disabled` that AC-31 expects can never be reached. A builder who checks in the listed order fails AC-31. The spec must say that the member's `disabled_at` is checked before the device's `revoked_at`, or AC-31 must change.
- F-2 · Major · spec.md, Push, the `withdraw` paragraph (line 187) and AC-34, against D-7 and D-12 · The design lets "another device of the same member" withdraw a memory. D-7 says "a memory belongs to the device that first sent it", D-12 says "memories by the device that first sent them", and neither row records a superseded clause. Because the Decisions table binds, a builder who follows D-12 would refuse that withdraw and fail AC-34. The spec must either record the widening in D-12's superseded-clause column or drop it from the design and AC-34.
- F-3 · Major · spec.md, Push, the `withdraw` paragraph (line 187) · The paragraph states two retry rules. The first says a retry whose base is "the revision just before a tombstone written by the same device or the same member" answers `withdrawn`. The last clause says "an item already withdrawn at the given base is `withdrawn` again", with no actor condition and with "at the given base" left undefined: it could mean the base equals the tombstone's revision or the one before it. A builder could write two different checks, and one of them would let any member's or device's stale withdraw pass as a retry. The spec must state one rule, with its base and its actor condition, once.
- F-4 · Major · spec.md, Sizing (line 95) · The paragraph opens "Measured by this part's tests", but the tests seed only the 100,000-row feed (AC-36). The 20,000 entries, 200,000 memory rows, storage figures and the 35-minute rebuild are estimates, not measurements. The storage sentence also contradicts itself: "at most 5 GiB of entry revisions … more with their revisions". An operator sizing the volume from "at most 5 GiB" would under-provision it. The spec must mark which figures are measured and which are estimated, and must give the revision storage without the "at most" bound. "Nothing else rests on size" is an unprovable absolute and should either name what was checked or go.
- F-5 · Major · spec.md, the "Subcommands, each recording itself in the audit with source `cli`" line (line 122) and AC-32 ("every CLI subcommand writes an audit row"), against line 120 and Restore detection (line 201) · The read-only subcommands (`status`, `members`, `invitations`, `audit`, `health`) are described as reads that run beside the server. Under these sentences they would also write audit rows, and every audit write must then update `feed.seq`. `serve health` may have no database open, and `serve status` must exit 1 when the database cannot be opened. A builder would either make the reads write or break AC-32. The spec must say which subcommands audit, probably the writers only, and AC-32 must match.
- F-6 · Nit · spec.md, the `/v1/health` behaviour (lines 154 and 134) against AC-31 and Risks "A full disk" (line 294) · The 503 when free disk is under 100 MiB appears only in an acceptance criterion and a risk. The design never states it, and neither does the limits section or D-15. The rule, and its effect on the image's `HEALTHCHECK`, belong in the design.
- F-7 · Nit · spec.md, Limits, rate limits, logs (line 211) · "the lease, mirror and maintenance timers cleared": no "maintenance timer" is defined anywhere, since pruning is deferred by D-25. The sentence should name the timer, probably the hourly bucket sweep, or drop the word.
- F-8 · Nit · spec.md, Limits, rate limits, logs (line 211) and Bootstrap (line 154) · "the bootstrap line is the only secret ever printed" is false as written: `serve invite` prints a code on standard output (line 124). The claim should be limited to the server's log, as line 154 already does ("the only secret the log ever carries").
- F-9 · Nit · spec.md, Non-functional requirements, Security and privacy (line 306) · "nothing but the payload types stored" overstates. The database also holds members, devices, invitations and audit rows with source addresses (D-8 says "plus member, device, revision, hash and times"). The sentence should repeat D-8's wording.
- F-10 · Nit · spec.md, Cost and time, Effort (line 99) · "the brainstorm's second round and the leads' round cut the mechanisms that would have doubled it" is an unprovable claim, and "the leads' round" is never defined. The clause should either name the mechanisms that were cut, as Alternatives does, or go.
- F-11 · Nit · spec.md, Sizing (line 95), Latency (line 305), Sources (line 11) · "the gate machine", "the leads' round" and "the six brainstorm lenses" are crew-process terms that the Terms section does not define. A reader new to the crew cannot tell what hardware the latency figures refer to. The machine should be named or defined once.
- F-12 · Nit · spec.md, Authentication (line 157) · "a habit after an exact lookup rather than a property" is opaque. It should say plainly that `timingSafeEqual` adds no security after an exact lookup by digest and is kept as a convention.
- F-13 · Nit · spec.md, Restore detection (line 201) · "with the 410 and the margin as the net for the clients that connect after the step and the window before it as the limit" does not say what the limit is. It should say which clients are unprotected, those that pull between the restore and `serve instance renew`, and what they miss.
- F-14 · Nit · spec.md, Risks, Join spam (line 291) · In "many addresses still grow both", the referent of "both" is unclear. It should name the audit table and the address buckets.
- F-15 · Nit · spec.md, Limits, rate limits, logs (line 211) · "which record what the server knows of 'why does my teammate's machine not see my entry'" is a conversational aside inside a field list. It should state the purpose directly, for example "enough to trace a missing item from the cursor, the filter and the refusals".
- F-16 · Nit · spec.md, Alternatives, "Entries keyed by the client's uid" (line 246) · The cited decision is D-20 (server-side validation), which says nothing about keying. The citation should be ADR 0024 D-8 alone, or D-8 of this spec.
- F-17 · Nit · spec.md, The command and the data directory (lines 118, 120), The mirror (line 205), Limits (line 211), Deployment (line 216) · Each of these paragraphs is one or two sentences of 150 to 400 words, chained by semicolons and parentheses, so a reader loses which clause a condition belongs to. They should be split into lists, as the Push rules already are.
