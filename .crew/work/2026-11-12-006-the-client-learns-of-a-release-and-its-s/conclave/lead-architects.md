## Notes
- `src/update/` outside `src/modules/` is the right place: it contributes nothing to a hook, has no tables and no rail entry, like `src/install/`, `src/settings/` and `src/status/`; ADR 0019's modules are for what answers the composer.
- `<data dir>/update.json` is right, not the brand level: `ui.json` is at the brand level because the app is shared by every product, while this state describes the binary in `<data dir>/bin`. It matches "the data directory holds the binary, the settings and the logs" (`src/util/paths.ts:25-28`) and `servers.json`.
- The check as a post-drain step is coherent with the existing guarded steps (`src/cli/commands/distill.ts:45-58`) and with `WIZARDINGCODE_MEM_DISTILL=off` (`src/cli/commands/hook.ts:48`). When no turn ends for days nothing checks, and `status`/`doctor` do not check themselves; the age in the line keeps that honest, but G-1's "within a day or two" (spec.md:17) should carry "of use" in ADR 0030's limits.
- `stageBinary`'s options are `{ version: string; force: boolean }` (`src/install/binary.ts:37`); spec.md:77 calls it with `{ version }` only. Pass `force: false` or make it optional.
- `reconcile` reads `stagedVersion`, then `stageBinary` reads it again (`src/install/binary.ts:46`), and `updateReport` a third time in `status`/`doctor`: up to three `--version` spawns, against "once" in spec.md:113 and :120. Consider passing the version already read, and skipping the spawn when the running copy is the staged binary.
- The new key forces more than `SETTING_KEYS`: a `RULES` entry (`src/settings/settings.ts:323`, typed by `Record<SettingKey, …>`), a field on `Settings`, and a default in `publicSettings` (`:441`). `GET`/`PUT /api/settings` will then carry it (`tests/unit/settings.test.ts:290`) while the viewer's `SettingKey` union (`ui/src/api.ts:136`) lacks it. Add the union member now (type only) or name the drift in the findings row.
- "Unknown value counts as off" is the opposite of `onOff`'s fallback-to-default (`src/settings/settings.ts:124`); deliberate and stated, but `mode(env, settings)` should read the merged value from `loadSettings` once rather than the environment a second time, so one precedence rule applies.
- ADR 0030 is free (the latest is `docs/adr/0029-release-distribution.md`). It should say it narrows ADR 0029's D-8 ("the client that learns of a release and follows it", `:30`) and that a channel copy is a new caller of `stageBinary`, not a sixth writer with its own rule.
- Cutting the viewer's About line and Settings field to the next item is coherent: no decision is removed (`conclave.md:20`), and spec.md:90 records the row in `docs/real-use/findings.md`. Only R-5 is left inconsistent meanwhile.
- `status` prints inside a `try` that needs the store (`src/cli/commands/status.ts:17-30`); the update lines are most useful when the store is refused, so print them from the data directory before or outside that failure path.
