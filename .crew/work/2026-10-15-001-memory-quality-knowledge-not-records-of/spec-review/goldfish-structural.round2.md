- F-1 · Major · (missing section) Alternatives considered · The feature rubric says a spec with no visible weighing of alternatives fails, and this one has no such section. The rejected options are scattered. Generative rewriting is deferred in Decisions as "a separate conversation" (line 29), not rejected for a stated reason. Risk a competent reader would miss: they cannot tell which designs were actually considered and which were never looked at, so they cannot confirm the design space was covered.
- F-2 · Major · (missing section) Risks and mitigations · The feature rubric requires failure modes, scale limits and what answers each, and Heilmeier Q5 asks for risks. No section collects them. The pieces are spread across sections 1, 5 and 6: knowledge loss is handled by the "0 keep lost" metric, false positives by the rule that a pattern is removed, and judge failure by "five failures stop the run". Some risks are never named at all. Section 5 (line 162) takes the drain lease for the whole run, but nothing says how long live distillation waits during a run of 2,513 memories. Nothing covers `--undo` once newer live memories have consolidated against the replacements, or after a second run. Risk a competent reader would miss: the lease blocking live distillation and the limits of undo.

What a competent reader would build wrong: following the stated rule, they would leave out `protocol-tag` and `verdict`, or the proof would flag them, which contradicts the table and the tests in §7.
