## Findings
- F-1 · Major · §4 Alternatives, row "A stricter threshold only, same questions" · "a record scores as high as a rule, so no threshold separates them" is stated as a fact, but nothing in the document measures it. Finding 2 shows that 79% of memories sit at importance 4 and that the question wording admits records. It does not compare the scores of records with the scores of rules, and the labels that could show it were lost (line 35). The cheapest alternative is rejected on a claim the reader cannot check. The proof already collects version 1 verdicts on labelled turns (§11), so it could test that alternative directly, and a reader would not think to ask for that test.
- F-1a · Nit · §4, row "The filter only, same questions" · "the three filter patterns matched 6 sample memories plus the 38 with host framing" adds a count from the 200-memory sample to a count from the 2,513-memory store (line 33). The two figures have different denominators, so the sentence makes the filter's reach look like one measurement when it is two.
- F-2 · Nit · §2 Goals · The goals are absolute ("A memory's title is a sentence that holds knowledge", "A turn that is only a record … stores nothing"), but §11 accepts 60% for titles and up to 15% for records. A reader who sees only §2 would expect every title and every record to meet the goal. Stating each goal with its §11 target would remove that gap.
- F-3 · Nit · §1 finding 3 · "A few signals" and "the hard cases are the mixed ones" are vague. "A few" means three (named in the parenthesis), and "hard" does not say what makes those cases hard. The second half of the sentence states the actual fact: the knowledge sits in one sentence next to the narrative.
- F-4 · Nit · §3 Principles and §4 row 1 · "(the founding rule)" and "Breaks the founding rule" point to a rule without saying where it is written. A reader new to the project cannot check it. Naming the ADR or document that states it would fix this.
- F-5 · Nit · §9, "Report penalties" and "A worth discount" · "a lower knowledge probability", "a lower `worth`" and "mostly carry report signals" give no amount and no share. "The amounts, the lists and the share are constants in the code" leaves the values to whoever builds it. D-6 only limits the outcome, so the spec cannot be checked against the values in the code.
- F-6 · Nit · §10, first journey · "then … the judge rates the second below `KNOWLEDGE_THRESHOLD`" puts a model's output in the "then". The third journey puts the judge's rating in the "when", which is the right place. Read as written, the first journey asks for a test that a live judge rates the sentence low, which no test can promise.
- F-7 · Nit · §10, "A turn the filter empties" · "an answer made only of a quoted hand-back" does not say whether "quoted" means inside an `<agent-message>` block (which `stripHost` removes) or quoted as prose. The outcome (no request, `skipped`) depends on which one is meant.
- F-8 · Nit · §1, §7 `worth`/`knowledge_<i>`/`kind`, §10 · The words "gate", "wave" and "lease" (§0 line 11), and "the hook snapshots and the perf budgets" (§13), are never defined. §0 defines the other terms. "Gate" also appears in the judge's criteria text, which a model reads with no project context. The Heilmeier rubric's first question asks for no jargon.
- F-9 · Nit · §3 D-7 · "the review runs a third round after the fixes of round 2" is a note about how the review is run, not a design decision. A reader of the design learns nothing about the feature from it.
- F-10 · Nit · §11 Cost · "[source?] for TypeSafe's price per token" correctly marks a gap. As a result, the document answers Heilmeier question 6 (cost) only in tokens, not in money. Question 7 (how long it will take) is not answered anywhere: no estimate of the build time or of the time a labelling and proof run takes.
- F-11 · Nit · Line 3 · "which found that what the mem keeps is a bigger problem than how it finds it" is a comparison with no measure. It is credited to three ADRs, but the line does not say which one supports it. §1 supplies the measure for "what it keeps" but not for "how it finds it".
