- F-1 · Major · "## 1. The noise filter (`src/distill/noise.ts`)", subsections "### The notes the session had been shown" (l.106) and "### The `Context:` line" (l.110) · These two subsections sit under a heading that names `src/distill/noise.ts` and opens with "Pure, no database". - F-2 · Major · Whole document, against spec-heilmeier.md questions 6 and 7 · Nothing says how long the work takes or what the proof costs. The proof needs 400 TypeSafe requests, a labelling agent over 400 memories, possible relabelling rounds, and the author checking 20 labels per sample. The only cost given is input tokens per live turn (section 6 metrics), and that is reported with no target. A reader can't judge whether the proof's labelling and relabelling loop fits the schedule or budget, and that loop gates the merge (l.234).
